**Stop hunting through Reddit threads for AliExpress promo codes.**

Coupon Wizard reads the total on your AliExpress checkout page, pulls the latest public
promo codes shared by the community (r/AliExpressCode), keeps only the ones valid for your
order size, and tries them for you one after another — keeping whichever gives you the
lowest price. If none work, it says so plainly: *"No coupon worked."*

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/sysmesh/coupon-wizard-for-aliexpress/releases"><b>Download the latest release</b></a> · Chrome Web Store listing coming soon</p>

## How it works

1. Add items to your AliExpress cart and go to **Checkout**.
2. Click the Coupon Wizard icon. It shows your cart total and the codes that match it.
3. Click **Find the best coupon** and watch it try each code, keeping the best one.

<p align="center"><img src="assets/screenshot-2.png" alt="Coupon Wizard trying codes" width="720"></p>

## Private by design

- No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no analytics.
- Nothing about your cart or your browsing is ever sent anywhere.
- The only network requests are to download the public coupon list.
- Only runs on the AliExpress checkout page. Full details in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## Support the developer (optional)

The popup has a clearly labelled *"Support us"* button. If — and only if — you click it,
your tab is routed through the developer's AliExpress affiliate link and brought straight
back to your cart. Same prices for you; a small commission helps keep the extension free.

## Install without the Web Store

1. Download `coupon-wizard-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/sysmesh/coupon-wizard-for-aliexpress/releases) and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, pick the unzipped folder.
